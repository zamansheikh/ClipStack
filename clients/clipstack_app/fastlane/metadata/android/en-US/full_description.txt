ClipStack brings your Mac or Windows PC clipboard to your Android phone — and back.

Copy something on your Mac and it's on your phone's clipboard a moment later, ready to paste. Share a photo, a link or a note from your phone and it lands on your Mac. No account, no cloud, no subscription: your devices talk directly over your own Wi-Fi, end-to-end encrypted.

★ HOW IT WORKS
1. Install the free ClipStack app on your Mac or Windows PC: github.com/zamansheikh/ClipStack
2. On the Mac, open Settings → Devices → Pair a Device. On the PC, click Pair a phone or Mac → Show pairing code.
3. Scan the QR code with ClipStack on your phone. That's it.

★ FEATURES
• Mac → phone, automatically: text, links, rich text and images you copy on your Mac go straight to your phone's clipboard.
• Phone → Mac in one tap: tap Send clipboard, use Share → ClipStack from any app, or just open ClipStack and it sends your latest copy.
• Screenshots straight to your phone: copy an image on your Mac and paste it into any chat.
• Phone to phone: pair two phones directly. One shows its pairing code, the other scans it.
• Windows PCs too: copies on the PC reach your phone automatically, text and images.
• Floating bubble: send your clipboard or paste from any app with one tap. Drag it to ✕ to hide it.
• One-tap paste (optional): turn on "ClipStack paste" in Accessibility and the bubble pastes straight into the field you're typing in.
• A beautiful history: every synced clip in a clean grid with filters for text, links and images. Tap to copy, hold for details.
• Works in the background: a quiet notification keeps the connection open, so Mac copies arrive while you use other apps.
• Light and dark themes, and a themed icon on Android 13+.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Clips travel only between your own paired devices on your local network.
• Every connection is end-to-end encrypted (X25519 + ChaCha20-Poly1305); pairing uses a one-time QR code.
• Nothing is uploaded and nothing is tracked. No ads, no account.
• Password-manager copies on the Mac are never captured or shared.

★ GOOD TO KNOW
• Needs ClipStack for macOS or Windows (free) on the same Wi-Fi network — or a second phone.
• Android only lets apps read the clipboard while they are open, so sending from the phone takes one tap (or the share sheet). Receiving from the Mac is automatic.
• Some guest, hotel and campus networks stop devices from seeing each other.

ClipStack is free and open source (MIT).
